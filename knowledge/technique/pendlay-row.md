# Pendlay Row (Тяга Пендлея)

## Setup

- Torso bent over to near-horizontal, more so than a typical bent-over row, held there for the whole set.
- Bar starts on the floor each rep (dead stop), grip similar to bent-over row.

## Cues

- Pull explosively from a dead stop on the floor to the lower chest/upper stomach, then return the bar all the way to the floor and let it fully stop before the next rep — no bouncing or touch-and-go.
- Keep the torso angle fixed and near-horizontal throughout, resisting the urge to stand up to help the pull.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "I stand up a bit to help each rep" → this defeats the purpose of the dead-stop, near-horizontal position — reduce the load until the torso angle can be held for every rep.
- "The bar doesn't fully stop on the floor between reps" → consciously let go of any residual tension and let the bar settle fully before resetting the pull.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Does the bar come to a complete stop on the floor between reps, or is there some bounce?

## Safety notes

- Same as bent-over row: the lower back is the main structure under demand from the bent-over torso position — not a great choice on a day the lower back is already fatigued or sore.
