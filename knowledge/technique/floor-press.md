# Floor Press (Жим штанги лёжа с пола)

## Setup

- Lying on the floor (or a low platform) with knees bent, feet flat.
- Same shoulder-blade retraction as bench press; grip similar to close-grip or standard bench depending on the goal.

## Cues

- Lower under control until the upper arms/triceps touch the floor — the natural depth stop for this exercise, no need to force further range.
- A brief pause at floor contact tends to happen naturally and is fine — press up from that dead-stop position.
- No leg drive is available the way it is on a bench, since the hips/legs aren't braced against a bench — press power comes from the upper body alone.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "My elbows hit the floor hard / feel jarred" → control the descent rather than letting the weight drop the last few inches; a rep lowered with control shouldn't produce impact.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Are the elbows touching down softly under control, or landing with impact?

## Safety notes

- Usually done with dumbbells or a bar handed off by a partner/rack set low; plan the un-rack/hand-off and the bail-out (rolling dumbbells off to the side, or a partner taking the bar) before starting a heavy set.
