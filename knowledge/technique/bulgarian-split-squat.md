# Bulgarian Split Squat (Болгарские выпады)

## Setup

- Rear foot elevated on a bench, front foot far enough forward that the front knee doesn't travel drastically past the toes at the bottom (some forward travel is normal and fine).
- Torso can stay upright or lean forward slightly depending on the goal (upright shifts more to quads, forward lean shifts more to glutes/hamstrings).

## Cues

- Lower under control until the back knee approaches (but doesn't need to touch) the floor.
- Drive up primarily through the front leg, using the back leg mostly for balance.
- Keep most of the weight on the front foot throughout.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "I feel unstable / wobble a lot" → reduce load and/or hold onto something for balance while building stability in the position — very commonly a balance-skill issue that improves with practice, not a strength issue.
- "My front knee hurts" → check front-foot distance from the bench — a front foot placed too close forces excessive forward knee travel; moving it further forward usually helps.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Is the limiter balance/stability, or leg strength/burn?

## Safety notes

- Have something nearby to hold onto (a rack upright, a pair of light dumbbells for counterbalance) while building confidence in the position, especially at higher loads.
