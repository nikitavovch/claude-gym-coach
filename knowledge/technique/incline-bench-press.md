# Incline Bench Press (Жим штанги лёжа на наклонной скамье вверх)

## Setup

- Same shoulder-blade retraction and foot-planting setup as flat bench, on a bench set to a moderate incline (commonly around 15–30°; steeper inclines shift more work to the front delts).
- Bar path and touch point shift slightly higher on the chest/upper chest compared to flat bench.

## Cues

- Same bar-path logic as flat bench — down under control to a consistent touch point, drive up and slightly back.
- Keep the shoulder blades retracted throughout; incline pressing tends to make this harder to maintain than flat bench, so it's worth extra attention.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "It feels much more like a shoulder exercise than a chest exercise" → check the incline angle — a steeper bench naturally shifts more load to the front delts; not necessarily wrong, but if more chest emphasis is the goal, lowering the incline angle is the lever to pull.
- "Same shoulder-pinch symptom as flat bench" → same response as flat bench — recheck scapular position and touch point.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Same as flat bench: where exactly is the discomfort, and at what point in the rep?

## Safety notes

- Same spotter/pin logic as flat bench, adjusted for the incline bench setup. Brace/breathe per `_general.md`.
