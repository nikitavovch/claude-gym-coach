# Leg Extension (Разгибание ног в тренажёре)

## Setup

- Seat and pad adjusted so the knee's pivot point roughly lines up with the machine's pivot, ankle pad resting just above the ankle.
- Back flat against the seat back.

## Cues

- Extend the knee through a full, controlled range, pausing briefly at the top without violently locking out.
- Lower under control rather than letting the weight stack drop.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "I feel a pinch at the front of the knee at the top" → reduce the range of motion slightly (don't fully lock out) and/or reduce load; if it persists across sessions, that's a "get it checked" flag rather than something to cue away.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Is the discomfort a pinch right at full extension, or a general ache through the whole movement?

## Safety notes

- Not a high-injury-risk machine in general use; the main note is not using momentum/bouncing the weight stack, which increases joint stress for no real benefit.
