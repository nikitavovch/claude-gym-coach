# Dip (Отжимания на брусьях (грудь))

*Also covers: Weighted Dip / Отжимания на брусьях с отягощением — same cues, added load.*

## Setup

- Hands gripping the parallel bars, arms supporting the full bodyweight at the top, shoulders down away from the ears rather than shrugged up.
- A slight forward torso lean is normal and shifts emphasis toward the chest; a more upright torso shifts emphasis toward the triceps — both are legitimate depending on the goal.

## Cues

- Lower under control to a depth the shoulders can tolerate without pinching — roughly until the upper arm is parallel to the floor for most people, but this varies by shoulder mobility.
- Keep the shoulders pulled down and back rather than letting them ride up toward the ears at the bottom.
- Press back up to full arm extension at the top without locking out aggressively/forcefully.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "My shoulders pinch or hurt at the bottom" → reduce the depth to a pain-free range, and keep the shoulders actively pulled down throughout rather than letting them shrug up as fatigue sets in.
- "It feels like a shoulder exercise more than chest/triceps" → check torso lean — a completely upright torso with the shoulders not actively engaged shifts more strain onto the shoulder joint itself; a controlled forward lean with engaged shoulder blades usually feels better and hits chest/triceps more directly.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Is the shoulder pinch happening at the very bottom of the dip, or through the whole range?
- Does the torso lean forward, stay upright, or vary rep to rep?

## Safety notes

- Shoulder pain on dips is one of the more common self-reported issues with this exercise — reducing range of motion and monitoring whether it resolves is the first, low-risk step; pain that persists despite reduced range and rest is a "see a professional" flag.
- For weighted dips, make sure the added weight (belt/vest) is secure before starting, and have a plan to step down/off safely if a rep can't be completed rather than dropping from height.
