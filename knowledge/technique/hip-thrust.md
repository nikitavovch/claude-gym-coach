# Hip Thrust (Ягодичный мост со штангой)

*Variant covered by this card: Machine Hip Thrust / Ягодичный мост в тренажёре.*

## Setup

- Upper back (around shoulder-blade height) supported on a bench, feet flat on the floor, knees bent roughly 90° at the top of the movement.
- Bar centered over the hips; a pad or folded mat between bar and hips is standard and worth using at any real working weight.
- Feet positioned so that shins are roughly vertical at the top of the rep.

## Cues

- Drive through the heels, extending the hips fully at the top — "squeeze the glutes hard" at the top of every rep is the main cue, since the exercise's whole purpose is finishing in full hip extension.
- Keep the chin tucked slightly and avoid hyperextending the lower back at the top — this should feel like hip extension, not lower-back extension.
- Lower under control back to the start rather than dropping.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "I feel it in my lower back more than my glutes" → check for over-arching at the top — tucking the chin and consciously squeezing the glutes (rather than pushing the lower back into extension) usually shifts the feeling back to the glutes.
- "My feet slide during the set" → recheck footwear/floor grip and foot position; flatter, grippier soles help.
- "The bar is uncomfortable on my hips even with padding" → try repositioning the pad, or a thicker pad — a comfort/tolerance issue more than a technique flaw, often improving with a few sessions of exposure.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- At the top of the rep, are you squeezing the glutes, or does it feel like the lower back is doing the finishing work?

## Safety notes

- Loading/unloading the bar over the hips is itself a small skill — roll the bar up over the thighs into position rather than lifting it directly onto the hips, to reduce awkward strain.
- No pin bail-out exists; if a rep stalls, simply stop pushing and let the hips lower back down under control.
- Brace moderately per `_general.md` — a full maximal-effort valsalva brace is not typically necessary here given the supported torso, but a general brace still helps.

## Variant notes

- **Machine Hip Thrust** — same setup priorities (foot placement, top-of-rep glute squeeze, avoiding lower-back hyperextension); the machine's fixed pad and path remove some of the bar-loading/positioning skill above. Don't carry a load or progression trend across from the barbell version — the two aren't directly comparable.
