# Lat Pulldown (Тяга верхнего блока широким хватом)

## Setup

- Thighs secured under the pad, grip slightly wider than shoulder width, arms fully extended at the start.
- Torso upright with a very slight backward lean, not a big layback.

## Cues

- Initiate by pulling the shoulder blades down before bending the elbows significantly.
- Pull the bar to roughly the upper chest/collarbone, keeping the torso mostly still (a slight lean is fine, a big swinging layback is not).
- Control the weight back up to a full stretch at the top.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "I lean back a lot and use my bodyweight to help" → reduce the load and keep the torso closer to upright, focusing on the lats/shoulder blades doing the pulling.
- "I feel it in my arms/biceps more than my back" → refocus on the shoulder-blade-down initiation cue before bending the elbows, and try a slightly wider grip if a narrower one is being used.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Does the torso stay mostly still, or does it swing back significantly to move the weight?

## Safety notes

- Low injury-risk machine; the main note is securing the thigh pad appropriately before loading heavy so the lifter doesn't get pulled up off the seat.
