# Push Press (Жим штанги с толчком ног)

## Setup

- Same starting position as a standing overhead press.
- Feet stable and flat, ready to absorb and produce force through a short dip.

## Cues

- Dip straight down a short distance (a few inches) by bending the knees, keeping the torso upright, then drive straight up explosively.
- Use the leg drive's upward momentum to help launch the bar, timing the arm press to take over as the legs finish extending.
- Same head-clearance and lockout bar-path cues as the strict overhead press.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "I bend forward at the hips during the dip instead of dipping straight down" → keep the dip shallow and vertical, like a quarter squat, rather than a hip-hinge.
- "The bar stalls partway up even with the leg drive" → check timing — the arms should start pressing as the legs finish extending, not wait until the legs are already locked out and momentum is lost.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Does the dip feel like a small squat, or does the torso pitch forward during it?

## Safety notes

- Same rack/bail-out logic as overhead press; because this moves more weight than a strict press, err toward safety pins set appropriately and a spotter for near-maximal attempts.
