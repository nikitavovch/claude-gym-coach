# Decline Bench Press (Жим штанги лёжа на наклонной скамье вниз)

## Setup

- Feet secured under the leg pads of a decline bench, same shoulder-blade retraction as flat bench.
- Touch point sits slightly lower on the chest than flat bench.

## Cues

- Same general bar-path cues as flat bench, adapted to the shorter range of motion this angle typically produces.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "Blood rushes to my head / I feel light-headed" → a normal effect of an inverted-ish position under exertion for some people — if uncomfortable, that's a reasonable signal to use flat or incline bench instead rather than push through it.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Does the light-headed feeling happen every time, or only on harder sets?

## Safety notes

- Getting in and out from under the leg pads with a loaded bar needs a plan — use a spotter for heavier sets, since escaping a failed rep is more awkward here than on a flat bench.
