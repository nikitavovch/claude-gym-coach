# Hack Squat (Гак-приседания)

## Setup

- Shoulders and back against the pads, feet placed on the platform (placement shifts emphasis — higher/forward for more glute/hamstring involvement, lower/back for more quad emphasis).
- Same "hips stay on the pad" principle as leg press.

## Cues

- Lower under control to the depth where the lower back stays supported against the pad.
- Drive through the whole foot to return to the top, without locking the knees out hard.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "My lower back rounds away from the pad at the bottom" → same fix as leg press — reduce range of motion.
- "My knees hurt tracking forward past my toes" → check foot placement — moving the feet slightly higher/forward on the platform reduces forward knee travel for lifters who find that uncomfortable, without it being strictly "required."

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Same as leg press — does the back stay against the pad throughout?

## Safety notes

- Same safety-catch awareness as leg press; check the specific machine's release mechanism before starting a working set.
