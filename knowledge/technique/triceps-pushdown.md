# Triceps Pushdown (Разгибание рук на блоке (трицепс))

*Also covers: Skull Crusher / Французский жим лёжа — different equipment, cues below note where they diverge.*

## Setup

- Pushdown: stand close to the cable stack, elbows pinned at the sides, slight forward torso lean.
- Skull crusher: lying on a bench, upper arms vertical and stationary, forearms starting bent with the weight near the forehead/behind the head.

## Cues

- Pushdown: extend the elbows fully without letting them drift away from the torso or flare out to the sides.
- Skull crusher: keep the upper arms still and vertical throughout, bending only at the elbow; lower the weight toward the forehead or just past it under control, then extend back up.
- Both: control the eccentric (lowering) portion rather than letting the weight/cable snap back.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "My elbows flare out / move around a lot" (pushdown) → reduce the load and consciously pin the elbows at the sides for the whole set.
- "My elbows drift backward toward my head" (skull crusher) → keep the upper arms vertical and stationary — drifting elbows usually means the load is being shared with the shoulders instead of isolated to the triceps.
- "I feel elbow discomfort" (either) → reduce range of motion slightly (don't fully lock out, or don't lower quite as far) and monitor across a few sessions; persistent elbow pain despite that adjustment is a "get it checked" flag.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Do the elbows stay in a fixed position throughout, or do they move to help the weight up?

## Safety notes

- Skull crushers in particular carry some risk of the weight drifting toward the face/head if the upper arms aren't kept stable — starting lighter than feels necessary while learning the groove is a reasonable precaution.
