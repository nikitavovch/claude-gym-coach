# Romanian Deadlift (Румынская тяга)

*Variant covered by this card: Stiff-Leg Deadlift / Тяга на прямых ногах.*

## Setup

- Start from the top (standing with the bar, e.g. off a rack or after a deadlift-style pull to standing), not from the floor.
- Soft knee bend, held roughly constant throughout — this is a hip-hinge, not a squat.
- Grip and stance similar to a conventional deadlift.

## Cues

- Push the hips straight back, keeping the bar close against the legs the entire way down.
- Keep the shins close to vertical — the knees bend only slightly more to accommodate the hips moving back, not to squat down.
- Stop the descent at the point where the lower back would otherwise round to go further — that point (often mid-shin to just below the knee) is that person's working range, not a fixed depth target.
- Reverse by driving the hips forward, squeezing the glutes at the top, not by yanking with the lower back.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "I feel it in my lower back more than my hamstrings" → check the range of motion — going past the point where the lower back stays flat is the most common cause; stopping a few inches higher and building range over time as hamstring flexibility improves usually shifts the feeling back to hamstrings/glutes.
- "The bar drifts away from my legs on the way down" → keep active contact between the bar and thighs/shins the whole way, similar to the conventional deadlift bar-path cue.
- "My knees bend a lot and it starts to feel like a squat" → re-cue "push the hips back" rather than "bend the knees," and consciously keep the shins close to vertical.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Where do you feel the working muscle — hamstrings/glutes, or lower back?
- Does the lower back round noticeably before you reach your usual working depth, or only near the very bottom of your range?

## Safety notes

- Lighter loads than a conventional deadlift are normal and expected — this isn't meant to be loaded the same as a floor pull.
- Set the bar down (or return it to the rack) rather than forcing a rep if the lower back starts to round past what feels controlled.
- Brace per `_general.md`, though the breath-hold is typically less critical here given the lighter relative loads usually used.

## Variant notes

- **Stiff-Leg Deadlift** — nearly identical, but knees stay closer to straight throughout (minimal bend) rather than a soft, slightly-bending knee. Increases hamstring stretch demand and usually needs a lighter load and a more conservative range — same "stop before the lower back rounds" cue, just earlier in the range for most people.
