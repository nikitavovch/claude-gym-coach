# Close-Grip Bench Press (Жим штанги узким хватом)

## Setup

- Same bench setup as flat bench, with a narrower grip (commonly just inside shoulder-width).
- Elbows tucked closer to the torso than a standard-grip bench.

## Cues

- Keep the elbows tracking close to the body through the descent and press, rather than flaring out.
- Same scapular retraction and leg-drive cues as flat bench.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "My wrists bend backward under load" → stack the wrist directly over the forearm (a straighter wrist), and consider wrist wraps if this persists.
- "My elbows hurt on the way down" → check the grip isn't excessively narrow (hands nearly touching) — a moderately narrow grip (just inside shoulder width) is usually enough to shift emphasis to the triceps without excessive elbow strain.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Is the discomfort in the wrist, the elbow, or the shoulder?

## Safety notes

- Same spotter/pin logic as flat bench press. Brace/breathe per `_general.md`.
