# Lateral Raise (Разведение гантелей в стороны)

## Setup

- Standing, dumbbells at the sides, a very slight forward torso lean is fine, soft bend in the elbows held constant.

## Cues

- Raise the arms out to the sides to roughly shoulder height, leading with the elbows rather than the hands.
- Keep the torso still — no using body momentum/swinging to help the weight up.
- Lower under control rather than dropping.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "I swing my torso or use momentum to start each rep" → reduce the load until the torso can stay still and the movement can be controlled from a dead stop at the bottom.
- "I feel it in my traps/neck more than my shoulders" → check for shrugging during the lift — consciously keeping the shoulders down away from the ears while raising usually redirects the feeling back to the side delts.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Does the torso stay still, or does it rock/swing to help move the weight?
- Do the shoulders stay down, or do they shrug up toward the ears during the lift?

## Safety notes

- Very low injury-risk exercise; no notable safety concerns beyond standard control of the weight.
