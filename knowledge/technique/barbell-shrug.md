# Barbell Shrug (Шраги со штангой)

## Setup

- Stand holding the bar at arm's length in front of the thighs, grip just outside the legs.

## Cues

- Elevate the shoulders straight up toward the ears, without rolling them forward or backward.
- Pause briefly at the top, then lower under control.
- Keep the arms relaxed and straight — this is a shoulder-elevation movement, not an arm movement.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "I roll my shoulders in a circle instead of straight up" → many coaches actively discourage rolling under load — cue a straight-up-and-down elevation instead, since rolling under heavy load is a common source of unnecessary shoulder-joint irritation for some lifters.
- "My grip gives out before my traps do" → straps are a reasonable tool here, since the exercise is meant to target the traps, not grip endurance.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Does the movement feel like a straight up-and-down shrug, or is there a rolling/circular component to it?

## Safety notes

- Low injury-risk exercise; the main note is avoiding a jerky, momentum-driven first rep with a heavy bar off the floor or rack.
