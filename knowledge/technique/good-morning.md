# Good Morning (Гудморнинг)

## Setup

- Bar positioned like a low-bar or high-bar squat across the back.
- Soft knee bend, feet roughly hip-width.
- Brace before initiating, same as squat/deadlift.

## Cues

- Push the hips straight back while keeping the knees only slightly bent, similar to a Romanian deadlift but with the bar on the back instead of in the hands.
- Keep the torso and lower back in their normal, controlled position — this movement punishes a rounding back more than most, since the load sits high on the torso.
- Go only as low as the lower back can stay controlled, then reverse by driving the hips forward.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "My lower back rounds before I get very far down" → reduce range of motion and load together — this exercise rewards starting conservative far more than most.
- "I feel unstable with the bar on my back at this angle" → use lighter loads than the squat, and consider a safety-bar or a spotter-supervised setup while building confidence in the position.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- How far down do you get before you feel your lower back wanting to round — barely past standing, or closer to horizontal?

## Safety notes

- One of the higher-risk-for-the-load exercises in the database precisely because a rounding back happens with a bar already loaded across the shoulders; conservative loading and range of motion are the main safety lever, more than any single cue.
- Use a rack with pins set to catch the bar if something goes wrong, and/or a spotter, especially when adding load.
- Stop well before the point where the low back rounds, not at the point where it starts to.
