# Overhead Press (Жим штанги стоя (армейский жим))

*Variant covered by this card: Seated Overhead Press / Жим штанги сидя.*

## Setup

- Grip just outside shoulder width, bar resting on the front delts/upper chest at the start.
- Feet roughly hip-width, stance stable enough to brace against.
- Brace before un-racking, same bracing logic as a squat.

## Cues

- Press the bar in as straight a line as possible; since the head is in the way, the common cue is to move the head back slightly ("get your head out of the way") as the bar passes the face, then push the head back through once the bar clears it — ending with the bar over the crown of the head, ears roughly between the biceps at lockout.
- Keep the glutes and abs braced throughout to avoid leaning back excessively to clear the bar — a very slight lean through the bar's path is normal, but the lockout position shouldn't be a pronounced backward lean held at the top.
- Driving through the legs is not part of a strict overhead press (that's a push press) — keep the feet flat and stable.
- Breathe and brace per `_general.md`, holding through the sticking point.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "I have to lean back a lot to get the bar past my face" → focus on the "move the head back, then push it through" sequencing, and check the brace is tight enough to prevent the lower back from compensating instead.
- "My lower back feels the strain more than my shoulders" → recheck the brace — a looser brace here often shows up as lower-back strain from an exaggerated lean.
- "My shoulders pinch near the top of the press" → check the bar path is finishing over the crown of the head (not still out in front) — a bar left in front at lockout increases shoulder impingement risk.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- At lockout, does the bar end up over the top of your head, or still out in front of your face?
- Is the strain you're noticing in the shoulders, or in the lower back?

## Safety notes

- Set rack pins/hooks at a height that lets the bar be racked without excessive reaching if a rep fails; if training without a rack, have a clear plan to lower the bar back to the shoulders and then to the floor/thighs if a rep can't be completed, rather than holding a failed lockout attempt.
- Brace/breathe per `_general.md`.
- Stop on shoulder pain distinct from normal pressing fatigue.

## Variant notes

- **Seated Overhead Press** — same bar path and head-clearance cue, performed seated (usually against a support or on a bench with back support), which removes the ability to brace through the legs/hips the same way a standing press does; expect the lift to feel less stable through the core for a given weight even though the arms/shoulders do similar work.
