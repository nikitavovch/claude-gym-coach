# Face Pull (Тяга к лицу)

*Also covers: Rear Delt Fly / Разведение гантелей в наклоне (задняя дельта) — different equipment, same intent.*

## Setup

- Face pull: cable set at roughly face/upper-chest height, rope attachment, stepping back to create tension before pulling.
- Rear delt fly: hinged forward at the hips (or chest-supported on an incline bench), dumbbells hanging below, slight bend in the elbows.

## Cues

- Face pull: pull the rope toward the face, splitting the hands apart at the end and aiming the elbows out and slightly up.
- Rear delt fly: raise the arms out to the sides, squeezing the shoulder blades together at the top, leading with the elbows.
- Both: control the return rather than letting the weight/cable snap back.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "I use momentum/body swing to help" (either) → reduce the load and focus on a controlled tempo through the whole rep.
- "I feel it in my traps more than my rear delts/upper back" → consciously keep the shoulders down (not shrugging) throughout, and for face pulls, focus on pulling the elbows out wide and up rather than just pulling the rope straight back.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Is the torso staying stable, or is it helping generate momentum?

## Safety notes

- Very low injury-risk exercises; no notable safety concerns beyond standard load control.
