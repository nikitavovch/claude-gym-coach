# Rack Pull (Тяга с плинтов)

## Setup

- Bar set on pins at a chosen height (commonly just below or at the knee), inside a rack.
- Same stance, grip and bracing setup as a conventional deadlift, adjusted for the bar starting higher.

## Cues

- Take the same slack-out-of-the-bar step even from the pins — don't yank from a completely dead, loose bar.
- Drive through the floor and finish tall, exactly like the lockout portion of a conventional deadlift.
- Keep the bar close to the legs through the (shorter) range of motion.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "I feel like I'm just leaning back to finish the rep" → focus on squeezing the glutes and standing tall through the hips rather than leaning the torso back past vertical at lockout.
- "My grip gives out before anything else" → since rack pulls commonly overload past a normal deadlift's weight, straps are a reasonable and common tool here, not a shortcut to avoid.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Is the sticking point right off the pins, or near lockout?

## Safety notes

- Rack pulls typically use heavier-than-deadlift loads over a short range — err toward a spotter or at least someone aware you're lifting near max. If a rep stalls, guide the bar back down to the pins rather than dropping it uncontrolled unless the equipment is rated for dropping.
- Brace/breathe per `_general.md`.
