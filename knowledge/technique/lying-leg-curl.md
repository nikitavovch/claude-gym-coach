# Lying Leg Curl (Сгибание ног лёжа)

*Represents the leg curl family: also covers Seated Leg Curl / Сгибание ног сидя and Standing Leg Curl / Сгибание ноги стоя — same cues, different machine orientation.*

## Setup

- Pad positioned just above the heel/Achilles, hips flat against the bench (for lying/seated versions).
- Grip the handles to stabilize the torso rather than letting the hips lift to help the movement.

## Cues

- Curl the heels toward the glutes through a full, controlled range.
- Keep the hips pinned down/stable throughout — avoid arching the lower back to help move the weight.
- Lower under control rather than letting the weight drop.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "My hips lift off the bench to help" → reduce the load until the hips can stay down for every rep.
- "I get a cramp in my hamstring or calf" → common with this exercise at higher reps; easing off the very top squeeze slightly or adjusting foot angle (pointing toes vs. flexing them) sometimes helps — not dangerous, just uncomfortable.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Do the hips stay flat/stable, or do they lift to assist the movement?

## Safety notes

- Low injury-risk machine in general; the main note is not to bounce the weight stack.
