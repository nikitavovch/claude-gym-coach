# Seated Cable Row (Тяга горизонтального блока)

## Setup

- Feet braced against the platform, knees softly bent, torso upright, arms extended to grip the handle at the start.
- Sit tall rather than rounded forward at the starting position.

## Cues

- Initiate the pull with the shoulder blades before the arms/elbows.
- Pull the handle to the lower stomach, keeping the torso mostly upright (a small amount of controlled lean-back at the end of the pull is fine, but rocking the whole torso back and forth to move the weight is not the intent).
- Control the return to a full stretch, letting the shoulder blades protract at the front of the movement.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "I rock my torso back and forth a lot to move the weight" → reduce load and anchor the torso more firmly, focusing on the arms/back doing the pulling rather than the torso's momentum.
- "My lower back feels it more than my back muscles" → check the torso isn't rounding forward at the start of each rep — sitting tall throughout the set addresses this in most cases.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Does the torso stay relatively still, or does it swing to help move the weight?

## Safety notes

- Low injury-risk machine; the main note is not letting the weight stack yank the arms into a sudden overstretch at the front of the movement — control the return.
