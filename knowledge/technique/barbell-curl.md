# Barbell Curl (Подъём штанги на бицепс)

*Also covers: Dumbbell Curl / Подъём гантелей на бицепс, Hammer Curl / Молотки — same cues, grip changes only.*

## Setup

- Stand with a stable base, arms hanging at the sides, elbows close to the torso.
- Grip: underhand/supinated for barbell and standard dumbbell curls, neutral (palms facing each other) for hammer curls.

## Cues

- Keep the elbows pinned close to the torso throughout — the upper arm should barely move; only the forearm swings.
- Curl through a full range without needing to swing the torso or heave the weight up.
- Lower under control rather than dropping the weight.

## Common errors (symptom → cue to try)

- "I swing my back/body to get the weight moving" → reduce the load until the elbows can stay pinned and the torso can stay still for every rep.
- "My elbows drift forward away from my body" → consciously keep the upper arms glued to the ribcage through the whole set, especially as fatigue sets in toward the last reps.
- "I feel it more in my forearms than my biceps" → check grip tightness (an overly tight grip shifts work to the forearms) and consider whether the weight is simply too heavy to control with the biceps alone right now.

## Self-diagnostic questions

- Do the elbows stay in roughly the same place throughout the set, or do they drift forward/back as it gets harder?

## Safety notes

- Low injury-risk exercise in general; the main note is avoiding a jerky, momentum-driven first rep with a heavy weight, which is where most curl-related strains happen.
